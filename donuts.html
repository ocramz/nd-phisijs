PhysiN · a link that needs 4 dimensions

Two solid donuts. The teal ring does not move. The maroon ring has two sliders.

In three dimensions you cannot link them. You must cut one ring, put the other ring through it, and join the ends again.

In four dimensions you do not cut anything. Move the maroon ring out of this 3-space with the w slider. It goes away from the screen, because the screen shows only the hyperplane w = 0. Move it along x. Then bring it back to w = 0. The rings are now linked, and the gauge shows that the two solids never touched.

Along the room. You can see this movement.
Out of this 3-space. The ring becomes thinner, then it goes away. The wire shape shows where it is.

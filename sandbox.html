PhysiN · rigid bodies in n dimensions

Each body is a solid in four dimensions. You cannot see a 4D solid. Thus the page cuts it with one 3D hyperplane. Drag the ruler at the bottom to move that hyperplane along the fourth axis, w. A band shows the interval of w that each body covers. The band has color when the hyperplane cuts that body.

This is the same library, with the dimension count set to three. The rotor becomes a quaternion and the angular velocity bivector becomes the usual axial vector. Thus you get a usual 3D engine as a special case.
